Tunissimo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Tunissimo – Piano Tuner ("we", "our", or "us") is made by Red Wolf Agency. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Microphone

Tunissimo uses the microphone to measure the pitch of the notes you play. Sound is analyzed on your device as you play. It is never recorded, saved or sent anywhere.

Your Pianos and Tuning Progress

The pianos you add, their measurements and your tuning progress are stored only on your device. We have no servers and never receive this data. Deleting the app deletes it.

Advertising

The free version of Tunissimo shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use data to show and measure ads, including:

  • Device identifiers, including Apple's advertising identifier if you allow tracking
  • IP address and general location (country or region)
  • App usage and diagnostic data

Before ads are shown, you are asked whether to allow tracking under Apple's App Tracking Transparency. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, you are also asked for consent under local privacy law. If you decline, you still see ads, but they are not personalized. Where the law requires it, you can change your choice later from "Ad privacy choices" on the app's home screen.

For how Google uses this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

The Remove Ads purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Once ads are removed, the ad service is no longer started.

Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted at this address with a new date.
